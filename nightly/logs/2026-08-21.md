The 9 NXDOMAIN results are spread over ~1,000 corpus rows with no three in adjacent or near-adjacent positions, so **the CLUSTER flag does not fire** and the queue notes are not prefixed.

**But the underlying disease is broader than last night's read.** Counting terse pattern-shaped hosts (`^[a-z]+bjj([a-z]{2})?\.com$`) across the corpus index:

```
rows 4800-4899:  50/100      rows 5300-5399:  57/100
rows 4900-4999:  52/100      rows 5400-5499:  46/100
rows 5000-5099:  73/100      rows 5500-5599:  58/100
rows 5100-5199:  48/100      rows 5600-5699:  68/100
rows 5200-5299:  71/100      rows 5700-5799:  44/100
                             rows 5800-5899:  75/100
                             rows 5900-5910:  10/11
```

**1,291 records corpus-wide carry this domain shape, and the last ~1,100 rows of the corpus are 44–75% composed of them.** Last night's finding — 9 of 11 NXDOMAIN in slice 00 sitting in rows 150–169 — read as one bad batch. Two slices in, it looks instead like a **systemic property of the corpus tail**: a large block of records whose URLs were pattern-generated (`<city>bjj.com`) rather than observed, of which a substantial minority never resolved.

Corroborating evidence from the existing queue (slice 00, 20 Aug): `alliancebjjspokane.com`, `flinflobbjj.com`, `galesburgbjj.com`, `janesvillebjj.com`, `laconiabjj.com`, `mankatobjj.com`, `nitrobjj.com`, `northedmontonbjj.com`, `ottumwabjj.com`, `saguenaybjj.com`, `springfieldbjjil.com` — eleven more of exactly this shape, from an independent slice.

**Recommendation:** before applying any of tonight's NXDOMAIN blankings, trace where corpus rows ~4800–5911 were sourced. If those URLs were synthesised from city names rather than observed, the fix is upstream and roughly a fifth of the directory's links may be affected. Blanking them one slice at a time will take a full 30-day cycle and will not address the cause. It is also worth noting the reverse risk: **`midlandbjj.com` and `deltabjj.com` both resolved to real, healthy BJJ schools in the wrong state** — pattern-generated domains do not only fail, they sometimes land on a genuine gym that is not the recorded one, which is a worse failure because it looks fine.
