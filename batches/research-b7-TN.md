## NOTES

- **Two known TN stubs both resolved as GONE, cleanly.** `athensbrazilianjiujitsu.com` and `knoxvillebjj.com` both returned DNS `Status: 3` (NXDOMAIN) — the strongest possible signal, exactly as the task description predicted. No further action needed on either beyond removal.
- **Repeated empty-body pattern.** Four different stored domains (chattanoogabjj.com, murfreesborobjj.com, kingsportbjj.com, and initially samuelbragabjj.com) all resolve via DNS but return a body with no text content at all — just a bare viewport meta tag. This looks like a shared web-builder/template quirk (possibly a JS-only renderer our fetch tool can't execute) rather than four coincidental "under construction" pages. I could not distinguish "real business behind a JS wall" from "abandoned domain" for these three (Chattanooga BJJ, Murfreesboro BJJ, Kingsport BJJ) and marked them UNVERIFIED rather than guessing. Wayback Machine and DuckDuckGo HTML fetches were both blocked/empty in this sandbox, which removed my usual fallback for this exact situation.
- **A genuinely surprising domain-squatting case:** the stored URL for "Kaze BJJ and Judo Institute" (Clarksville, TN) is a live, well-built site — but it is a *different, real business* on the other side of the continent (Scarborough, Ontario, Canada), confirmed by reading that business's own /location.html subpage with a Canadian postal code. This is exactly the "working link is not evidence the record is right" trap called out in the method rules, and it would have sailed through any DNS-only or reachability-only screen.
- **A genuine city mismatch inside TN, not a URL failure:** "Gracie Jiu-Jitsu Foothills" is stored under Seymour, TN, but the business's own page gives its address as Maryville, TN and describes Seymour only as one of several towns it serves. Separately, a completely different, genuinely Seymour-based school ("Seymour Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Academy," seymourbjj.com, est. 2017, instructor Jon Dysert) was found and is NET-NEW. Recommend correcting the stored city for Gracie Jiu-Jitsu Foothills to Maryville and adding Seymour BJJ Academy as its own Seymour record.
- **tbjja.com (Tennessee Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Academy, Spring Hill) is running a compromised WordPress instance** — its "Actualités récentes" sidebar lists hundreds of German-language online-casino SEO spam post titles injected by what looks like a hacked plugin or theme. The core school page itself (address, instructors, testimonials) is intact and unaffected, so the REAL verdict stands, but this is worth flagging to the site owner separately as it's an unrelated but real problem with that domain.
- **Budget/context statement:** WebSearch was used 15 times this pass (after the two method files and the live-page fetch), concentrated on (a) triaging the 8 no-URL stub records, (b) chasing three empty-body domains, and (c) opportunistic breadth in a handful of additional towns (Franklin, Oak Ridge, Greeneville, Elizabethton/Sevierville). I did not attempt a systematic sweep of the rest of Tennessee's ~90+ incorporated cities — Dyersburg, Tullahoma, Shelbyville, Manchester, Winchester, Fayetteville, Pulaski, Dickson, Gallatin, Portland, White House, Mount Juliet, La Vergne, Sevierville proper, Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Morristown, Rogersville, Newport, Sweetwater, Dayton, Athens (now closed — replacement not sought), Paris, Milan, Kingston, and the Memphis suburbs (Bartlett, Collierville, Germantown, Millington) were not individually checked. Rows affected by this scope limit: everything in the ROSTER is solid (own-page read), but the roster is very likely still well short of the "roughly double" heuristic the method file describes for other regions — Tennessee is large and this pass prioritized fixing the 34 existing records and confirming their stubs correctly over exhaustive new-city discovery. A follow-up pass focused purely on the untouched middle/west/east-TN small towns would likely find several more NET-NEW schools.
- No DC-style city-string requirement or NL-style hardcoded-city-list issue applies to Tennessee, and no blank-override name collisions (Jungle Gym Martial Arts, Action & Reaction MMA, Ethos BJJ, Alliance Jiu Jitsu Easley, Ironside Martial Arts) were encountered among the TN names above.
