- **The 810 H St NE ambiguity (Capital City BJJ vs. Underworld BJJ)** — both hstbjj.com ("Capital BJJ," legacy-looking content) and underworlddc.com ("Underworld BJJ," clearly current content) independently claim the identical street address, 810 H St NE, Washington, DC 20002. Rather than guess which one is the live, current occupant (or whether both somehow co-exist), Underworld BJJ is proposed as the NET-NEW record (stronger current-content signal) and "Capital City BJJ" is left as an open WRONG-URL/UNVERIFIED verdict rather than silently resolved. A human reviewer with the ability to call the phone numbers or check a more recent capture should make the final call before either is published, to avoid either dropping a real school or publishing a duplicate under two names at one address.
- **DC BJJ Academy** — held back with a GONE/UNVERIFIED verdict rather than being replaced with a guessed match, since no candidate business by any name surfaced in two separate targeted searches.
