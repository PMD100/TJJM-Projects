## NOTES

- **Discipline-mismatch pattern**: this region had an unusually high rate of NOT-BJJ verdicts among stored records — Eclipse Martial Arts, Krav Maga Anchorage, Shoshin Ryu, and Alaska Center For The Martial Arts all read as legitimate, real, operating martial-arts schools that simply do not teach Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Japanese jujutsu, Krav Maga, or generalist TKD/Hapkido programs instead). Tonbo Dojo and Shoshindo of Alaska are very likely the same pattern but could not be confirmed on a primary source because Facebook blocked direct reads — flagged NOT-BJJ-leaning rather than a hard NOT-BJJ.
- **JBER base-access restriction**: Arctic Warrior Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a real, active, well-documented program, but its own page states classes are open only to those with JBER installation access (service members, qualifying family members, DoD civilians) — not the general public. Per the batch addendum's explicit instruction, this is flagged rather than silently kept or silently dropped; a human editor should decide whether "JBER-access-only" schools belong in a general-public directory.
- **Domain squat**: ikaalaska.com is the clearest "GONE" case in this batch — the domain fully resolves and returns a real page, but that page is unrelated Indonesian-language content with zero Alaska or martial-arts connection. This is a domain-expiry/resale case, not a business closure narrative that could be traced further.
- **JS-rendering limitation**: Several sites (graciebarraalaska.com, evojj.com root, cjjusaalaska.com) returned near-empty bodies via the fetch tool despite resolving fine in DNS — almost certainly client-side-rendered (React/Squarespace dynamic) sites that need a real browser to display content. Where this happened, I used a specific sub-page that did render (e.g., evojj.com/about-us-2) when available, and otherwise marked the specific unread claim (usually the address) as UNVERIFIED rather than inventing or carrying forward a third-party number as if I had read it myself.
- **Budget note**: I did not exhaustively search every small Southeast Alaska or Bush community (e.g., Utqiagvik, Bethel, Cordova, Dillingham) given the shared WebSearch budget constraint flagged in the method rules; Sitka got one search pass and was held back rather than added on thin evidence. If more budget becomes available, Southeast Alaska (Juneau/Ketchikan/Sitka corridor) and the Bush are the weakest-covered areas here.
- Every verdict above states the specific page and text I actually read; where I only had third-party/aggregator evidence I said so explicitly (Gracie Barra Alaska's address, Team Caique's address/lineage, Evolution Jiu Jitsu's street address, Tonbo Dojo's and Shoshindo of Alaska's discipline, Gracie Jiu-Jitsu North Pole's entire listing).
